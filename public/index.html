Grading scale

  • D (2.00) is the minimum pass. D or higher earns the course credits.
  • F (below 40%) and I (incomplete) are worth 0.00. Both count in your GPA and stay on the transcript for good.
  • Absent from a final you were due to sit counts as zero marks, F.
GPA  = sum(credits x grade point) / total credits
CGPA = the same, over every course in semesters 1 to 8

Semester GPA uses normal rounding. The final CGPA always rounds up when the 3rd or 4th decimal is non-zero. Example: 3.4510 becomes 3.46.

05 / Section 2.4, backlog and improvement

Retakes and the B ceiling

  • Who: anyone who failed a course or skipped the exam after paying fees. A student who failed a course, promoted or not, can also improve any course below B, in any semester.
  • When: only with the immediately following odd or even semester batch.
  • What: you resit the final exam only. Your old class test and attendance marks are kept.
  • Ceiling: the recorded grade is B (3.00) at most, whatever you score. The grade sheet is marked improved, or irregular for a failed student.
  • No downside: if the retake grade is worse, your old grade stays. If the new grades don't raise your GPA, the old GPA stays.
  • Labs: a failed or missed lab can be retaken once, with the next batch and Academic Committee permission. Otherwise F is recorded.

06 / Section 2.6

Improving CGPA after graduating

  • Only semester 7 and 8 courses where you got below B.
  • Once only, with the immediately next batch.
  • Within 6 years of your first admission session.
  • The same B (3.00) ceiling applies to each course.
  • Your transcript and certificate show improvement and the exam year.
  • If the new grades don't raise your CGPA, the old result stays.
  • You can apply, with a fee, to cancel the improved CGPA and restore the old one. The application goes through the Chairman and your hall provost to the Controller of Examinations.
07 / Section 2.5

Degree requirements

  • Final CGPA of 2.25 or higher
  • At least 154 of the 160 credits earned. A course earns its credits at grade D or above.
  • Every core course passed
  • All semesters finished within 6 academic years
  • Any industrial or professional training the department requires (CSE 710 Industrial Training sits in semester 7)

08 / Sections 2.3 and 2.6

Struck off or dropped out

Dropped out, permanently

  • Missing the 2.00 GPA, then failing again in the retake, or not taking it.
  • Missing the exam twice in one semester, or more than twice across the program.
  • Going past the 6-year limit.

Name struck off, readmission possible

  • Unpaid fees, or missing the final for low attendance: readmit with the next batch.
  • GPA under 2.00, or skipping an exam after paying fees: readmit before the next semester with the next batch.
  • Disciplinary removal: apply once the suspension ends.
  • Apply within 1 academic year. Attendance is counted from the readmission date.
09 / Section 3.4

Credits by semester

Theory (code ends in an odd digit) Lab, project, thesis (even digit)

115 theory + 45 lab = 160 credits. A 3-credit theory course meets 3 hours a week. A 1-credit lab meets 2 hours a week. Options I to III in semesters 6 to 8 are electives. CSE-coded courses are core.
